## Supplied claims and corrections

Checked Gemini's consequential attributions against primary documentation:

- BP5 is RP2040, not an FTDI-MPSSE instrument; older USB transport is a different role.
- GreatFET One is LPC4330/SGPIO, not an onboard FPGA engine; Saleae Pro uses FPGA sampling.
- MAX2870 is a synthesizer; HackRF's actual components differ by model/revision.
- FT4232H has two MPSSE channels plus UART/bit-bang resources, not four identical engines.
- Application-specific hardware can be useful; supported modes and discarded
  information determine suitability. Promiscuous packet output is not universal I/Q.
- URH/Netzob offer relevant inference patterns; original URH is now archived.
  Inspected selected source engines, not claimed universal recovery or runtime fitness.
- Wireshark Lua and Scapy are relevant interpretation/packet-tool references,
  not selected dependencies, hardware frontends or state-semantics substitutes.
